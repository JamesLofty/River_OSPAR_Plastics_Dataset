### OSPAR_meta_analysis.xlsx
Workbook containing the meta-analysis inputs used in the study.

## Synthetic data
### synthetic_data_all.xlsx
Workbook containing the material and geometric properties of all **229,196 litter items** across River-OSPAR categories.
Includes:
- Mass  
- Volume  
- Density  
- Geometric dimensions:  
  - L1 (longest dimension)  
  - L2 (intermediate dimension)  
  - L3 (shortest dimension)
---
### synthetic_data_top_25.xlsx
Workbook containing the material and geometric properties of the **top 25 River-OSPAR litter categories**, representing approximately **80% of all river litter**.
Includes:
- Mass  
- Volume  
- Density  
- Geometric dimensions:
  - L1
  - L2
  - L3
---
### top_25_plastics.xlsx
Workbook containing:
- Counts of the top 25 River-OSPAR plastic categories
- Percentage contribution of each category based on the meta-analysis
